# 1. Vision

Simple Todo exists because the people who most need reminders are the ones most penalized by the friction of creating them. Every second spent tapping through fields, picking a category, or setting a priority is a second where the task might just stay in your head — and usually does.

This app collapses task capture to a single voice utterance. The user speaks naturally, the AI parses the intent, a confirmation screen catches errors, and the task is saved with reminders already scheduled. From that point on, the app does the remembering. The user does not need to return, review, or curate — they just receive the push notification at the right time and act.

The honest differentiator is design priority, not feature count. Everything in this app — the voice-first entry point, the preset reminder offsets, the minimal task anatomy — subordinates organization to recall. The goal is a reliable memory prosthetic the builder actually reaches for.

The project carries a second purpose with equal weight: a deliberate learning vehicle for the BMad Method, producing planning artifacts (this PRD, architecture, stories) intended to serve as reusable templates for future projects. Both purposes are real — the app must be genuinely useful, and the artifacts must be genuinely instructive.
